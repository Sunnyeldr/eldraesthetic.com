Artist profile

A voice of fire. A world of feeling.

RYNVA brings a raw edge to ELDR ÆSTHETIC. Her identity is immediate: copper hair, unfiltered expression and a sense of strength without excess.

Alongside SUNRIVA she is part of the collaborative EP SHARDS. The two artists find common ground in a sound that moves between vulnerability and resolve.
