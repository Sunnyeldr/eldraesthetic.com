Artist profile

A voice of feeling. A spirit of strength.

SUNRIVA gives Nordic-inspired music an intimate, emotional pulse. Airy atmospheres, clear melodies and powerful vocal moments create a sound that can feel fragile in one line and fearless in the next.

Her collaborative EP SHARDS with RYNVA includes Komdu Heim and Ég man þig, moving between resolve and the longing for home.
